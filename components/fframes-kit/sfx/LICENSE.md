checked for CC0 on 2026-10-25, when the file was downloaded. The words of the dedication:

Each file was cut to its sound (silence below -60 dB removed at both ends, at most 1.9 s),
mixed to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz, set to a sample peak of -3 dBFS, given a 20 ms fade
at its end and saved as 16-bit WAV with ffmpeg. Credit is not required; the authors are
named here as thanks.
