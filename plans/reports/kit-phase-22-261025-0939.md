## Caption files (4f99217f)

- `CaptionWord` has the fields of Remotion's `Caption`:
  - every reader fills `timestamp_ms` with the middle of the word or cue;
  - subtitle cues have `confidence` 1.
- **SRT.** `parse_srt` follows Remotion's lenient mode:
  - it accepts a byte order mark and CRLF line endings;
  - it accepts `,` or `.` in times, and the hours are optional;
  - a cue ends at a blank line, and cues without text are dropped.

  `serialize_srt` writes one cue per line of words, joined without a separator.
- **VTT.** fframes' own WebVTT parser (`fframes::media::Vtt`) rejects cue identifiers
  (`parsing error at 5:24, looking for Digit`). `parse_vtt` therefore checks the `WEBVTT`
  header and reuses the SRT scanner. That scanner skips identifiers and NOTE, STYLE and
  REGION blocks.
- **Whisper.**
  - `from_whisper_json` reads the openai-whisper shapes, `segments[].words[]` and the API's
    `words[]`, and the whisper.cpp shape, `transcription[]` with `offsets`.
  - Word text keeps its leading space.
  - The whisper.cpp shape comes from its `-oj` output (`timestamps`, `offsets`, `text`); it
    was not checked against a live run.
- **`serde_json`** became a regular dependency. It was optional and enabled only by
  `studio`. `components/Cargo.lock` did not change: the lock diff was identical before and
  after.

## Word captions (3a0c8da3, revised in 84889f1d)

- **`tiktok_pages`** follows Remotion's rule: a word with a leading space starts a page once
  the page has run past `combine_within_ms`. Tests cover `combine_within_ms` of 0 (a page
  per word) and 1200, words without spaces, and the empty input.
- **First render.** The text was too small and the pages popped as they changed. The
  default style is now the title style, and each page fades out over `exit` (0.1 s) before
  the next one starts. Pops went from 3 to 0.
- **Word layout.** A popped phrase cue overlapped its neighbour. Words are now placed by
  their current, popped widths, and lines wrap at `width / pop`, so a line still fits while
  its word pops.

## Text fitters (2e23ef34)

- `fit_text_on_n_lines` uses a binary search over sizes up to `style.size`.
- The first version summed word widths, and a line measured whole came out 0.00006 px wider
  than `max_width`. Each line is now measured whole.
- The test "never overflows" runs 5 boxes with a fake monospaced measure. It checks that
  each line fits, the line count, that the text is unchanged, and that size + 1 does not
  fit.
- `fill_text_box` tests cover a word added to a line, a word on a new line, a word that does
  not fit (it is not added), and a word wider than the box.
- The kit has no font fixture for unit tests. The real measurement runs through the gallery
  scenes.

## Voiceover timing (d2dcb57d)

- Bookmarks are timed by counting letters and digits, not words. A test with a transcript
  that joins two words of the text ("circle,then") still puts each mark on the right word.
- The tests cover:
  - the times of `in_sequence` blocks;
  - bookmark times;
  - `time_until_bookmark` and `remaining`;
  - subcaptions in container time;
  - one track per block;
  - a block without words.

## Gallery and docs (84889f1d)

- **Deviation from the plan.** The plan put the Whisper JSON and the SRT in
  `gallery/media/`, but `include_media_dir!` panics on `.srt` and `.json`. `.json` is
  Lottie with the `lottie` feature, which the asset-pack phase enables in the gallery. The
  files therefore live in `components/gallery/captions/` and are read with `include_str!`.
- **`Voiceover`.**
  - The scene times a circle and a square from the bookmarks of an SRT block timed to
    `tones.wav`.
  - The first version had a "s left" counter that popped every frame (11 findings) and
    drew a circle with `r = 0` (an inspect warning). The counter became a shrinking bar,
    and the circle is skipped until it has a radius.
- **Docs.** `components.md` has a "Fitting text into lines" section, a `WordCaptions` row
  and a "Word captions and voiceover timing" section.
- **Mapping rows.** `lib.rs` maps the names of `@remotion/captions`, the Whisper converters,
  `@remotion/layout-utils` and manim-voiceover. The speech services are marked "not
  available".
