# Kit phase 6: explain layer and math kit, progress

Status: in progress. Todos 1 and 2 are done; todos 3 to 7 are still open.

## First slice: space, axes, graphs

- **`explain::Space`** (Manim's frame): it has units with `y` up, an origin in pixels and pixels per unit.
  - `Space::FULL_HD` is 1920x1080 at 135 px per unit, 8 units tall, with the origin in the middle.
  - `Ui` has a new `space` field. `Ui::new` and `Ui::for_scene` start with `FULL_HD`; it is never derived from the video size. `Ui::shifted` copies it.
  - `Ui::with_space` and `explain::Canvas` give children another space.
- **`layout::Anchor`**: moved out of `latex`. `fframes_kit::latex::Anchor` still works as a re-export, and the prelude exports it without the `latex` feature.
  - It gained `direction`, `opposite` and `next_to` (Manim's `next_to(point, UR, buff)`).
- **`label::Label`**: one line of text placed next to a point, with `FadeIn`/`FadeOut`. The text is measured with the font, or estimated at 0.55 em per character when the font is missing.
- **`explain::{NumberLine, Axes, NumberPlane}`**:
  - `Range::new(min, max, step)`;
  - the mapping is the `Copy` struct `AxesMap`, with `c2p`/`p2c`, separate from the drawing props;
  - the axes cross at 0, or at the end of a range that does not contain 0. A number where the other axis crosses is left out, as in Manim;
  - lines and ticks are `Shape`s, so `Create` and the other shape animations apply. Numbers and labels fade through `labels_enter`/`labels_exit`;
  - `NumberPlane` has a grid at every tick and `faded_line_ratio` subdivisions.
- **Builders on `Axes`**:
  - `plot` (gives a `FunctionGraph`), `vertical_line` (a `DashedLine` down to the x-axis), `graph_label`, `riemann_rectangles` and `area` (with `bounded`);
  - every result keeps the axes' `AxesMap`, so it can be placed without the axes.
- **`FunctionGraph`**:
  - `f` is a plain `fn(f64) -> f64` (`Func`);
  - the samples are data-space `(x, f(x))`, cached in a process-wide map. The key is the function's address, the range bits and the count; the map starts over at 512 entries;
  - the pixel mapping is applied every frame, and the curve is smoothed as Catmull-Rom Béziers;
  - a value that is not finite, or more than 10^4 ranges away, breaks the curve (`1/x`, `tan`).
- **Gallery** (`gallery/src/scenes/explain.rs`): `NumberLine`, `NumberPlane` and the two Manim ports, `SinAndCosFunctionPlot` and `GraphAreaPlot`, with `<text>` labels. I compared the PNGs with Manim's reference images by eye. The structure matches: the curves, the vertical line at 2π, the Riemann strip at 0.3..0.6, and the area between the curves from 2 to 3. The colours come from the theme.
- **Not changed:** the feature `math` and its crates (glam, num-complex, statrs) are not added yet. Nothing in this slice needs them.

## Unresolved

- **The fast curve draw is reported as a pop.** A 7000-pixel cosine drawn by `Create` moves about 100 px per frame even over 2.2 s, so `--pops` flags the regions it enters in a single frame. It is motion, not a pop. The gallery scene draws over 2.2 s instead of 1 s.
- **Graphs need a capture-free `fn`.** A graph whose function depends on an animated value (a tangent at a moving `x`) needs either a sampling path without the cache or a closure type. That comes with the tangent/secant figure.
- **Labels are `<text>`.** MathTex labels for the axes wait for the `TexEngine` memory map that the advisor proposed (`prepare` in the constructor, `cached` per frame).
