# Kit phase 6: explain layer and math kit, progress

Status: in progress. Todos 1 to 4 are done. Todo 5 is under way, with figures 1 to 4 of 12 done; todos 6 and 7 are still open.

## First slice: space, axes, graphs

- **`explain::Space`** (Manim's frame): it has units with `y` up, an origin in pixels and pixels per unit.
  - `Space::FULL_HD` is 1920x1080 at 135 px per unit, 8 units tall, with the origin in the middle.
  - `Ui` has a new `space` field. `Ui::new` and `Ui::for_scene` start with `FULL_HD`; it is never derived from the video size. `Ui::shifted` copies it.
  - `Ui::with_space` and `explain::Canvas` give children another space.
- **`layout::Anchor`**: moved out of `latex`. `fframes_kit::latex::Anchor` still works as a re-export, and the prelude exports it without the `latex` feature.
  - It gained `direction`, `opposite` and `next_to` (Manim's `next_to(point, UR, buff)`).
- **`label::Label`**: one line of text placed next to a point, with `FadeIn`/`FadeOut`. The text is measured with the font, or estimated at 0.55 em per character when the font is missing.
- **`explain::{NumberLine, Axes, NumberPlane}`**:
  - `Range::new(min, max, step)`;
  - the mapping is the `Copy` struct `AxesMap`, with `c2p`/`p2c`, separate from the drawing props;
  - the axes cross at 0, or at the end of a range that does not contain 0. A number where the other axis crosses is left out, as in Manim;
  - lines and ticks are `Shape`s, so `Create` and the other shape animations apply. Numbers and labels fade through `labels_enter`/`labels_exit`;
  - `NumberPlane` has a grid at every tick and `faded_line_ratio` subdivisions.
- **Builders on `Axes`**:
  - `plot` (gives a `FunctionGraph`), `vertical_line` (a `DashedLine` down to the x-axis), `graph_label`, `riemann_rectangles` and `area` (with `bounded`);
  - every result keeps the axes' `AxesMap`, so it can be placed without the axes.
- **`FunctionGraph`**:
  - `f` is a plain `fn(f64) -> f64` (`Func`);
  - the samples are data-space `(x, f(x))`, cached in a process-wide map. The key is the function's address, the range bits and the count; the map starts over at 512 entries;
  - the pixel mapping is applied every frame, and the curve is smoothed as Catmull-Rom Béziers;
  - a value that is not finite, or more than 10^4 ranges away, breaks the curve (`1/x`, `tan`).
- **Gallery** (`gallery/src/scenes/explain.rs`): `NumberLine`, `NumberPlane` and the two Manim ports, `SinAndCosFunctionPlot` and `GraphAreaPlot`, with `<text>` labels. I compared the PNGs with Manim's reference images by eye. The structure matches: the curves, the vertical line at 2π, the Riemann strip at 0.3..0.6, and the area between the curves from 2 to 3. The colours come from the theme.
- **Not changed:** the feature `math` and its crates (glam, num-complex, statrs) are not added yet. Nothing in this slice needs them.

## Second slice: groups, layout, labels, simulation

- **`layout`**:
  - `arrange(sizes, direction, gap)` (any of the eight directions), with `row` and `column` as shorthands;
  - `grid(sizes, columns, gap)`: a column is as wide as its widest box, a row as tall as its tallest;
  - every arrangement is centered on the origin;
  - `safe_area(area, margin)` and `Anchor::within(area, w, h, margin)`;
  - `Anchor::toward(dx, dy)`, the nearest of the eight directions;
  - `separate(boxes, gap)`: moves each box below the earlier ones it overlaps, in order. That is the "simple overlap avoidance".
- **`explain::VGroup`** (shapes moved and animated together):
  - `arrange` and `arrange_in_grid` use the layout functions and keep the group's center;
  - `shift`, `move_to` and `bounds`;
  - one `enter`/`exit`/`emphasis` for the whole group.
- **`label`**:
  - `Label::bounds`;
  - `spread(ui, labels, gap)`, which moves labels down with `separate`;
  - `Leader`: a line from a point (with an optional dot) to a label. The label sits on the side the line points to; the line draws with `enter` and the text fades with `label_enter`;
  - the dot-and-elbow callout from phase 4 is still `overlay::Annotation`, and the module docs point to it.
- **`sim::Sim`**:
  - `precompute(dt, steps, initial, step)` stores `steps + 1` states;
  - `sample(t)` interpolates linearly between the two states around `t`, and clamps before 0, after the end, and on `NaN`;
  - a `dt` that is not positive gives a simulation that never leaves `initial`;
  - `Lerp` is implemented for `f32`, `f64`, `Point`, `Vec2`, pairs, arrays and `Vec`.
- **`explain::ParametricFunction`** (todo 2's requirements list it):
  - it is a `Curve = fn(f64) -> (f64, f64)`, drawn in units of `space`, or in data units of `axes` when they are set;
  - `ParametricFunction::new(ui, f, t_range)` takes `ui.space`, and `Axes::plot_parametric` puts it on axes;
  - it shares the graph's sample cache, with a tag in the key.
- **Gallery**:
  - `Canvas`: two canvases, each with its own origin and 130 px per unit. A Lissajous curve is in the left one's units and axes are in the right one. This covers `Ui::with_space` and `Space::frame`, which nothing had drawn with before;
  - `VGroup`: a row of five shapes and a 3-column grid of dots;
  - `Leader`: three leaders on a curve, and three labels placed at nearly one point and then spread;
  - `Sim`: a damped pendulum stepped at 240 Hz for 4 s in a `OnceLock` and sampled every frame.

## Third slice: matrix, table, brace labels, value tracker

- **`explain::Matrix`** (Manim `Matrix`): `<text>` entries on `layout::grid` between square brackets, centered on `(x, y)`.
  - A shorter row is padded with empty entries, and a matrix without entries draws nothing.
  - The brackets are shapes, so they draw with `enter` (`Create`). The entries fade with `entries_enter`, which defaults to the theme's `FadeIn`.
  - `cell(ui, row, column)` gives the area of an entry, to put a highlight behind it, and `bounds(ui)` the box around the brackets, to place what comes next to the matrix.
- **`explain::Table`** (Manim `Table`): the same grid, with optional `col_labels` and `row_labels` in `label_style`.
  - The lines run through the middle of the gaps between the columns and rows; `outer_lines` adds the frame.
  - `cell(ui, row, column)` is the area of a body cell between its lines (Manim `get_cell`).
  - Matrix and table share a private `TextGrid`, which places measured cells and gives the span of every column and row.
- **`label::BraceLabel`** (Manim `BraceLabel`): the existing `shapes::Brace` with a `Label` at its `tip()`. The label goes on the side the tip points to, and is centered on the point for a brace of zero length.
- **`anim::ValueTracker`** is `Anim<f32>`. The doc comment maps Manim's `set_value` animations to `timeline!` keyframes, `get_value()` to `ui.sample`, and `always_redraw` to `render_frame`.
- `label::text_size` is the measurement `Label::size` used, shared now with the cells.
- **Gallery**:
  - `Matrix`: a 3x3 matrix with "A =" placed by its `bounds`, a vector and a highlighted entry;
  - `Table`: Manim's labelled table with a highlighted cell;
  - `BraceLabel`: the width and height of a rectangle;
  - `ValueTracker`: a dot on a parabola that follows a stored timeline, with its `x` as a number.
- **Deviation from the plan:** `Canvas` takes a whole `space: Space` (built with `Space::frame`) instead of `unit: f32`. A space also needs its origin, and one value cannot place two canvases side by side.

## Fourth slice: the first math figures

Todo 5 is in progress: figures 1 to 4 of 12 are done. Their docs and the README line are in.

- **The kit's own doc example** for formulas in `docs/components/components.md` now compiles without the `latex` feature: its code sits in a module behind `cfg(feature = "latex")`. Before this, `cargo test -p fframes-kit --features math` failed on it.
- **Feature `math`**:
  - `fframes_kit::kits::math`, behind `cfg(feature = "math")`;
  - its prelude exports sit behind the same feature;
  - `docs/components/math.md` is doctested when `math` is on. The gallery enables `math`, so the editor bridge's wasm check covers it.
  - No crates were added.
- **Most figures are overlays.** They take the mapping of the line or axes they sit on and draw only themselves. `FunctionPlot` is the exception and draws its axes. The animated values are `ValueTracker`s.
- **Figures**:
  1. **`NumberPoint` and `Interval`.** `NumberPoint` is a dot at a tracked value on a `NumberLine`, with a label that can write the value. `Interval` is a thick segment with a filled dot at a closed end and a ring at an open one.
  2. **`FunctionPlot`.** It draws axes, a graph and its label. Its tracer is a dot that follows a tracked `x`, with the dashed vertical line and the point's coordinates.
  3. **`Tangent` and `Secant`.**
     - The tangent's slope is a central difference in pixels. A `Tangent` is a `Line` between computed points, so it needs no closure.
     - The secant goes through `x` and `x + dx`, with Manim's dashed `dx`/`dy` legs.
     - When the two points meet, the secant takes the tangent's direction, so `dx -> 0` ends on the tangent.
     - Where the function is not finite, neither line is drawn.
  4. **`RiemannSum` and `AreaUnder`.**
     - `RiemannSum` steps through a list of widths and cross-fades from one to the next. The sum fades with the rectangles, so it is not flagged as a pop.
     - `RiemannRectangles` got `sum()`, which shares the rectangle loop with its path.
     - `AreaUnder` reveals an `Area` left to right with a clip path, using `ui.id` and a `key`. This keeps the sample cache from being refilled every frame. It writes the integral of the part shown so far.
     - `math::integral(f, g, a, b, n)` is the trapezoid rule they use.
- **Refactor:** the enter/exit fade helper moved from `explain::axes` to `anim` (crate-private), so the kits can use it.
- **Gallery** (`scenes/math.rs`): `NumberLinePoint`, `FunctionPlot`, `TangentSecant`, `RiemannSum`, `AreaUnder`. I reviewed an 8-frame `strip` of each.

## Unresolved

- **The fast curve draw is reported as a pop.** A 7000-pixel cosine drawn by `Create` moves about 100 px per frame even over 2.2 s, so `--pops` flags the regions it enters in a single frame. It is motion, not a pop. The gallery scene draws over 2.2 s instead of 1 s.
- **Graphs need a capture-free `fn`.** The tangent and secant did not need a closure, because they are lines between computed points. A graph whose shape depends on an animated value still needs one, or an uncached sampling path. That is deferred until `Epicycles` needs it.
- **Labels are `<text>`.** `Matrix` and `Table` cells are `<text>` for the same reason. MathTex labels for the axes wait for the `TexEngine` memory map that the advisor proposed (`prepare` in the constructor, `cached` per frame).
