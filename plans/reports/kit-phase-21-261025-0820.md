## CS additions (25dcfb9e, 84383ab9, df57d636)

These were built in parallel in a separate worktree by a subagent, cherry-picked onto main
without conflicts, and gated again on main.

- `GraphView`:
  - curved edges;
  - backward edges, the edges that close a cycle in a depth-first search from node 0, curve
    round the forward ones;
  - weights on a plate at the curve midpoint;
  - `Graph::layout(&GraphLayout, scale)` with the shared graph layouts.
- `TreeView`: `TreeLayout::Tree` through `layout::tidy_tree`.
- `ArrayView`: `ArrayTrace::record` with `append` and `pop`, laid out for the longest step;
  a `label`.
- `Stack`: `indices` and `label`.
- `VariableBox`: manim-dsa's `MVariable`.
- Nine manim-dsa rows in the `lib.rs` mapping table.
- Tests with `--features cs`: 283 unit and 68 doc tests passed on the branch. The plan's
  "weight labels sit on the curve midpoint" is among them.
- `inspect --pops`: no problems in WeightedGraph, VariableBox, Dijkstra, GraphView, Stack,
  TreeView or Heap. `ArrayView` (frame 21325) and `BinarySearch` (frame 21869) warn with and
  without the additions, so those warnings predate this phase.
- The subagent expected the Dijkstra picture to change. No e2e frame shows Dijkstra, and
  every existing snapshot stayed at 0.000%.
