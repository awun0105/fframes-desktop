# Kit: SVG filter effects

Date: 2026-10-24. Machine: the Windows Server VM, CPU only, `--renderer cpu`.

## Result

Done. The colour, edge, noise, pattern, overlay, texture, halftone, light, geometry, reveal
and blur groups are in `components/fframes-kit/src/effects/`, each with a gallery scene and
an e2e frame. The docs list the per-pixel and displacement effects as not yet available, and
map `makeTransform` and `interpolateStyles` with a doc example.

Checks on 2026-10-25: `cargo test` in `components/` (353 kit unit tests, 104 doctests, all
passing), a test that no two effects share a filter kind (59 kinds, so one key never gives
two effects the same id), `e2e cpu` and `e2e skia-cpu` within the limit, `check-wasm`,
`clippy -D warnings`, and `boundary` with only the known workspace changes.

## Blur cost (CPU backend, 1080p, 28 threads)

`render <scene>` of the 5 second bench card (150 frames), wall-clock including start-up and
encoding, the median of three runs:

| Scene | Runs (s) | Median (s) | Against `BenchKit` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `BenchKit` | 0.93, 1.05, 1.05 | 1.05 | 1.0 |
| `BenchRegionBlur` | 1.80, 2.11, 1.74 | 1.80 | 1.7 |
| `BenchLinearProgressiveBlur` | 7.39, 7.26, 7.42 | 7.39 | 7.0 |
| `BenchRadialProgressiveBlur` | 7.07, 7.38, 7.57 | 7.38 | 7.0 |

The progressive blurs at their defaults (0 to 50) draw the sharp card and eight blurred
copies of the whole area: about 42 ms more per frame, so about 5 ms per blurred copy. The
region blur adds one blurred copy and a mask.

## Renderer findings

- Small `<pattern>` tiles drift between the backends; the pattern overlays draw paths, one
  per colour, instead.
- `feImage` of an element (`href="#id"`) matches on both backends.
- In an arithmetic composite, `k4` and subtraction change the alpha too; the chains keep
  their inputs opaque (weights summing to 1, an inverted copy instead of a subtraction).
- `feTurbulence` carries noise in its alpha; it is turned into an opaque grey before it is
  combined.
- Skia clips every shape on its own, so two clips meeting on a line leave a seam and
  `shape-rendering="crispEdges"` on a clip does not help. `Mirror` and `Tile` lay a larger
  copy underneath and mask the one on top.

## Unresolved

- The displacement effects wait for a `svgr` with the `feDisplacementMap` fix
  (linebender/resvg#1157). When it arrives, check the e2e difference per effect.
- The per-pixel effects (fisheye, pixelate, chromatic aberration, zoom blur, page turn and
  the others) need code that runs per pixel.
- The editor draws the filters through the browser; e2e does not cover how they look there.
