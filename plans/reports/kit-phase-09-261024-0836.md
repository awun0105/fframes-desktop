# Kit phase 9: physics and chemistry kits, progress

Status: in progress. Todo 1 (the dependency checks) is done, and physics figures 1 to 3 of 12 are in.

## Dependency checks

I checked these in a scratch crate before writing any figure on them.

- **`chematic` 1.2.1 exposes 2D coordinates** (open question 2 of the math report), so no SVG has to be parsed.
  - `chematic::smiles::parse` and `chematic::depict::compute_depict_data` give atoms (element, position, label, CPK colour, charge) and bonds (single, double, triple, aromatic, wedges).
  - The units are about 40 per Å. Phenol came out as a regular hexagon with the O on one vertex.
  - `chematic` is a facade crate. The kit needs the features `smiles` and `depict` only.
  - `threed` (`chematic-3d`) is not needed: `Molecule3D` takes XYZ input as the strategy says, and XYZ is simple to read.
- **`mendeleev` 0.8.4** has the symbol, name, atomic weight and group of each element.
- **`rapier2d` 0.36.1 with `enhanced-determinism`** builds and steps natively. Its `step` takes a `SoftBodySet` since 0.36. All three crates build for `wasm32-unknown-unknown` (release).
- **Decision: no `rapier2d` for now.** The plan allows it only where no closed form exists and says `GasBox` *may* use it. A seeded hard-sphere stepper inside `Sim` is deterministic by construction and needs no crate. The advisor agreed. If `GasBox` needs more, rapier goes behind the sub-feature `physics-rapier`, as the Risks section says.
- **`TexTemplate::physics()` exists** (phase 5).

## First slice: projectile, pendulum, spring

- **Shared pieces:**
  - **`motion::Rng`** is SplitMix64 with `next_u64`, `unit` (in (0, 1]) and `range`. It was lifted from `math::normal_samples`, which now uses it with the same bits, so the snapshots are unchanged. It is there so `physics` and `chemistry` do not depend on `math`.
  - **`kits::short`**, the number formatter, moved up from `kits::math` for the same reason.
- **Feature `physics`:**
  - `fframes_kit::kits::physics`, with prelude exports behind the feature. It adds no crates.
  - `docs/components/physics.md` is doctested when `physics` is on, and the gallery enables it.
- **Conventions** (written in the module docs and at the top of `physics.md`):
  - figures are placed in units of `ui.space`, with `scale` units per meter;
  - quantities are SI, and `GRAVITY` is 9.81;
  - each figure has its own clock: local time minus `delay`, times `time_scale`;
  - the fixed parts draw with `enter`, the moving parts fade with `parts_enter`;
  - a figure whose motion data is missing draws nothing.
- **Simulations:** RK4 (`physics::rk4`), 240 steps a second, stored in a `Sim` that is built once. Explicit Euler would gain energy and fail the pendulum's conservation test.
- **Figures:**
  1. **`Projectile`** is a closed form, so it has no `Sim`. It draws:
     - a dashed trace behind the ball;
     - the velocity and its two components. They fade out over 0.25 s after the landing, because a landed ball is at rest;
     - the peak with a dashed drop to the ground and `h = … m`, and `R = … m` under the landing point. Both fade in when they are reached, which keeps `--pops` quiet.

     `flight_time`, `position`, `velocity`, `apex` and `range` are public. A ball thrown flat or downward never flies.
  2. **`Pendulum` with `EnergyBars`:**
     - `PendulumMotion::new(length, angle0, seconds)`, or `::damped(length, angle0, damping, seconds)`, is `θ'' = -g/L sin θ - c θ'`. Its methods are `state`, `energy(t, mass)` (the potential is 0 at the lowest point), `amplitude` and `small_angle_period`.
     - The pendulum draws a ceiling, a dashed rest line, a dashed arc of the swing's reach, the rod, the bob, and an angle arc with its value in degrees.
     - The value sits on the bisector of the angle, so it moves with the angle. The first version anchored it left or right of the rest line, and `--pops` flagged the jump at 0°. At small angles the value now touches the rod.
     - `EnergyBars` takes plain values: kinetic, potential and their sum, the sum stacked from the two colours. You feed it `motion.energy(t, mass)` at the figure's clock. Zero-height bars are left out, so the renderer does not warn about them.
  3. **`SpringMass`:**
     - `SpringMotion::new(stiffness, mass, x0, seconds)` / `::damped(..)` is `m x'' = -k x - c x'`. Its methods are `state`, `energy`, `period` and `trace(until, per_second)`.
     - It draws a wall, a floor, a dashed rest line, a zigzag spring and the block.
     - With `plot: Some(axes.map(ui))` it draws the displacement so far as a `LinePlot` on those axes, with a dot at the current value.
- **Gallery** (`scenes/physics.rs`):
  - `Projectile`: 11 m/s at 0.95 rad, 3.5 s;
  - `Pendulum`: 2.6 m from 0.9 rad, with the bars, 6 s;
  - `SpringMass`: k 20, m 1, c 0.6, 5.5 s. The scene ends when the plot's 5 s of axes end; at 6 s the block ran on past the last plotted point.
