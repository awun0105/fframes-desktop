## Slice 1: presentations

- **Remotion names, checked on remotion.dev on 2026-10-24:**
  - `flip()` from `@remotion/transitions/flip`: the exiting slide turns 180° and shows the next one on its back. Options: `direction` (`from-left`, `from-right`, `from-top`, `from-bottom`) and `perspective` (1000).
  - `clockWipe()` from `@remotion/transitions/clock-wipe`: the exiting slide is wiped out in a circular movement, uncovering the next one. Options: `width` and `height` of the video.
  - `iris()` from `@remotion/transitions/iris` (since 4.0.316): the next scene shows through a circle that opens from the middle. Options: `width` and `height`.
  - All three also take the four `outer`/`inner` `Enter`/`Exit` style options. The kit has no CSS, so it leaves them out.
- **Flip, ClockWipe and Iris**, through the `Presentation` trait:
  - `Flip` is drawn flat, as a scale about the middle, because SVG transforms are affine. So there is no `perspective`, and `direction` only picks the axis. One side shows at a time, the exiting one in the first half.
  - `ClockWipe` clips the entering item to a wedge that sweeps clockwise from twelve o'clock. This looks the same as wiping the exiting item away on top.
  - `Iris` clips the entering item to a circle that grows to the far corners.
  - Each takes a `size` like `Slide` and `Wipe`.
- **Ported from the examples:**
  - `CircleWipe`, from the white disc in `examples/marketing`: a disc of `color` grows from `center` (a share of the area) until it covers the exiting item, then the entering item opens from the same point.
  - `SlantWipe`, from the chapter wipe in `examples/vinuni-future-rises`: a slanted panel with a stripe in front and one behind sweeps across. It covers the area at the middle of the transition, and the entering item is behind it. Its colours and `skew` are props; the theme gives the background, highlight and accent colours.
- **Gallery:** `Flip`, `ClockWipe`, `Iris`, `CircleWipe` (from 70 %, 40 %) and `SlantWipe` (with its back stripe in the title colour, so it shows against the accent panel), in the 2.2 s two-panel pattern of `Wipe`.
- **Frame review:** a circle of radius 0 is invalid SVG and was reported on the transition's first frame. `Iris` now draws nothing there, and `CircleWipe` draws nothing or only the colour.
- **`inspect --pops`:** the new transitions trip the pop check in the middle of the transition, like the `Slide` and `Wipe` scenes do. The phase 3 report explains why: a hard edge that crosses flat panels at this speed always does. Only `Fade` is clean. No transition pops on its first or last frame.
- **e2e:** the plan names `CircleWipe@50%`. At 50 % of the scene the disc exactly covers the area, so the frame is flat colour. The e2e frame is taken at 1.25 s instead, with the entering item opening over the colour.
- **Tests:**
  - the iris reaches the corners;
  - the wedge sweeps clockwise from twelve and covers the corners at the end;
  - one side of the flip faces the viewer at a time;
  - the circle covers and then opens from an off-centre point;
  - the slant panel starts left of the area, covers it at the middle and ends right of it.
- **Gates:** clippy with all features on the workspace passes.

## Slice 2: effects

- **`Trail` and `CameraMotionBlur`** (Remotion's `@remotion/motion-blur`): both render their children again at earlier local times.
  - `Trail` draws `samples` copies `spacing` seconds apart, fading linearly from `opacity`.
  - `CameraMotionBlur` spreads `samples` moments over the time the shutter is open (`shutter_angle` / 360 of a frame). It draws each over the earlier ones with opacity 1/k, so all count the same where the children are opaque.
  - Copies are capped at `MAX_SAMPLES` (16). A copy before the local start is skipped, not clamped to 0, so the trail grows in without a repeated first frame.
  - The earlier times come from `Ui::earlier`: a `Ui` at 8 times the frame rate, with the index and duration scaled to match. Children that count in local seconds see the right time. `frame.global_index` keeps the video frame, and the docs say so.
- **`LightLeak`** (Remotion's `<LightLeak>`, with `seed` and `hue_shift`): four warm radial gradients in screen blending. The kit's `Rng` (SplitMix64) places them past the top or bottom edge and sets their drift. Each flares up and fades along a half sine from its own start, over `length` seconds or the container's length. Screen blending was checked on the CPU backend: the light brightens the picture.
- **`Blur`**: a wrapper that blurs its children with a Gaussian whose `radius` can be animated. It is a wrapper because a blur belongs to what it wraps. `Glow` and `DropShadow` stay what they are, filter definitions drawn once and named by many elements. They already shipped in `decor` and are not duplicated here.
- **`Noise`**: film grain from `feTurbulence` turned grey, with the seed moving every frame when `animated`. Two consecutive frames were compared on the CPU backend, and the grain differs between them.
- **`ShaderNoise`** (feature `skia`): the grain as `SkSL`, one random grey per `scale` pixel cell. It needs the Skia backend, so the CPU backend draws nothing and the gallery uses `Noise`.
  - `tests/shaders.rs` compiles the shader with `fframes_skia_renderer::render::compile_shader`, checks its uniforms, and checks that a broken copy is reported.
  - The renderer is a dev-dependency only, with the gallery's Skia features (`vulkan`, or `metal` on macOS). Without them no prebuilt Skia matches, and the source build needs Python, which this VM does not have.
  - The kit's `skia` feature stays a plain gate, so users of the library never build Skia for it.
- **Gallery:** `Trail`, `CameraMotionBlur`, `LightLeak`, `Blur` (a focus pull) and `Noise`, plus `BenchTrail` (the bench card behind an eight-copy trail) after the other bench scenes.
- **Frame review:**
  - **`Blur` in linear light:** the blurred panel came out a shade off its colour, and much darker with a dark halo round the text at some radii (about 1.5 and 0.2, while 1.1 and 0.3 were fine). The converted SVG showed that `color-interpolation-filters="sRGB"` on the `<filter>` element does not reach its primitives, so the blur ran in linear light. The attribute now sits on `feGaussianBlur`, and the panel keeps its colour within one level.
  - **The same issue in `Glow` and `DropShadow`:** the kit's existing filters (`decor/filters.rs`) set the attribute on `<filter>` too, so it does not reach them either. Fixing that changes their snapshots and lies outside this phase, so it is recorded here for a later change.
  - **Fast motion:** at first the ball and the spinning bar moved so fast that they crossed a 64 px tile in one frame, and `inspect --pops` flagged them. That is the motion itself, not the effects. The scenes now move slower (the ball over 3.6 s, the bar at 300° a second with a 360° shutter) and still show the effect.
- **`inspect --pops`:** all five effect scenes are clean. `onion Trail -n 6` shows the trail along the wave.
- **Cost:** `BenchTrail` against `BenchKit`, three five-second renders each on the CPU backend: 997, 980 and 963 ms against 540, 641 and 391 ms. The medians are 980 and 540 ms, so an eight-copy trail takes about 1.8 times as long as the whole render, encoding included. `components.md` says so.
- **Tests:**
  - the trail copies fall back and fade;
  - the shutter spreads samples before the frame and caps them;
  - the light leak flares up and fades out, the same for the same seed and different for another;
  - `hue_shift` turns orange light blue;
  - the grain moves only when animated;
  - the two shader compile tests.
- **Totals:** all features 313 unit and 90 doc tests; `skia` 118 and 27 plus the 2 shader tests; `biology` 150 and 41; no features 118 and 27.
