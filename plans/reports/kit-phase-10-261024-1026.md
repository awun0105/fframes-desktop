# Kit phase 10: computer science and biology kits, progress

Status: in progress. The layouts are done, and CS figures 1 to 6 of 13 are in: `ArrayView`, `SortVisualizer`, `BinarySearch`, `LinkedList`, `Stack`/`Queue` and `TreeView`.

## Dependency checks

I checked `rust-sugiyama` in a scratch crate before writing any figure on it.

- **`rust-sugiyama` 0.4.0** builds natively and for `wasm32-unknown-unknown`. Its API is `from_edges(&[(u32, u32)], &Config) -> Vec<(Vec<(id, (x, y))>, width, height)>`.
  - The output was identical in five separate processes, so no `RandomState` leaks into it.
  - It accepts cycles.
  - Connected parts come back as separate layouts.
  - Nodes without edges are missing from the output.
  - `width` and `height` count vertices; they are not coordinates. Coordinates are multiples of `vertex_spacing` (10).
  - Only node coordinates come back, with no positions for the dummy vertices of long edges.
- **`Config::new_from_env` reads environment variables.** The wrapper always passes `Config::default()`, so frames cannot differ between machines.
- **`petgraph` is not taken directly.** `rust-sugiyama` brings it, and the CS figures record their own step traces (BFS, DFS, Dijkstra), which petgraph's algorithms do not expose.

## First slice: layouts, arrays, sorts, binary search

- **Features:** `cs` and `biology` both turn on `dep:rust-sugiyama`, because the biology food web is a layered graph too. `layout::layered` is gated on `any(cs, biology)`; `tidy_tree` needs no crate and is always there. The dependency is in `components/Cargo.toml`.
- **`layout::tidy_tree(children, root)`:**
  - subtrees are placed left to right, each shifted until its left contour clears the right contour of the ones before it, one slot apart at every depth;
  - parents sit midway between their first and last child, and the leftmost node is at x = 0;
  - a node listed under two parents belongs to the first, and cycles and unreachable nodes cannot loop.
- **`layout::layered(count, edges)`:** the Sugiyama layout normalised to layers (`y`) and slots (`x`).
  - Connected parts sit side by side, one slot apart.
  - Nodes without edges follow in layer 0.
  - Self-loops and edges to missing nodes are dropped.
- **`ArrayTrace`:** algorithms run once and record `ArrayStep`s. Each step has the values, the compared cells, where each cell came from (`moved`), the sorted cells, named pointers, the range in play, a found cell and a note.
  - The recorders are `bubble`, `insertion`, `merge` (bottom up, merged in place by rotation so every step holds each value once), `quick` (Lomuto) and `binary_search`.
  - `at(seconds, step_time)` samples the trace. `totals` counts comparisons (steps that compare without moving) and moves.
- **`ArrayView`:**
  - cells with values and indices;
  - state colours blend from the previous step;
  - moved cells travel on arcs, right over the top and left underneath, and are drawn above the rest;
  - pointers slide, stack on a shared cell and animate their row;
  - the notes fade out, then the next fades in;
  - light fills get dark text.
- **`BinarySearch`:** an `ArrayView` with "looking for …" above it. When the target is found, the range closes to the found cell.
- **`SortVisualizer`:** bars as tall as their values, the name, and a running count of comparisons and moves.
- **Gallery:**
  - `ArrayView`: a reversal by two pointers, from hand-built steps, one step a second;
  - `SortVisualizer`: the four sorts racing on twelve values, a step every 0.07 s;
  - `BinarySearch`: 23 in ten values.

## Second slice: lists, stacks, queues and trees

- **Shared helpers:** figures whose items keep their identity share step sampling (`StepAt`) and tweening by id.
  - Items in both steps slide.
  - New items come from an offset and fade in; items that went leave by an offset and fade out.
  - Items that pass another moving item (their order along x flips) swerve to the left of their way, so two items trading places pass each other, and are drawn on top.
  - Links fade in and out by id pair; tree edges match their reverse.
  - `ArrayView`'s pointers and the step notes moved into the same helpers. Its e2e frames are unchanged at 0.000 %.
- **`ListTrace`:** items with ids, explicit links when they are not "each to the next", highlights, a found item, pointers and a note.
  - The `ListRecorder` operations: `push`/`pop`/`peek`, `enqueue`/`dequeue`/`peek_front`, `insert`/`remove` (`cur` walks to the node before), `find`, `reverse` (`prev`/`cur`/`next`, one link turned back a step, then the new order) and `note`.
- **`LinkedList`:** nodes with a value and a link cell, and a `head` marker.
  - A link is a cubic whose bend follows the horizontal distance, so it goes continuously from a straight arrow (forwards) to a loop under the nodes (backwards) and never jumps while nodes move.
  - A slash marks a node without a link.
- **`Stack`:** a container `capacity` boxes tall with a `top` marker; pushes drop in and pops rise out.
- **`Queue`:** a channel with `front`/`back` markers; items enter from the right and leave to the left.
- **`TreeTrace`:**
  - binary trees, laid out once per step with `tidy_tree`; a single child gets an empty sibling slot so it leans to its side;
  - `bst` with `insert`, `search` and `remove`, where a node with two children is replaced by the smallest node on its right;
  - `max_heap`/`min_heap` with `push` (sift up) and `pop` (last node to the root, sift down). Nodes keep their values, so a swap trades places.
- **`TreeView`:** round nodes; the path walked is outlined, highlighted nodes light up and a found node turns green. Each step is centred on its own width.
- **Gallery:** `LinkedList` (insert, find, remove, reverse), `Stack`, `Queue`, `TreeView` (insert 35, search 60, remove 30) and `Heap` (push 45, pop).
