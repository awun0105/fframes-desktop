# fframes-kit, basic transitions: measurements

Date: 2026-10-23. Windows Server VM, 32 threads, CPU renderer (`--renderer cpu`), release build.

## Result

`fframes_kit::transitions` has `TransitionSeries` with `sequence` and `transition` items,
the `Presentation` trait with `Fade`, `Slide` and `Wipe`, `Timing::Linear` and
`Timing::Spring`, `SceneFade`, and `scene_transition` for core scenes with an `Overlap`.
The gallery has the scenes `Fade`, `Slide` (spring timing), `Wipe`, `SceneFade`, and
`BeforeOverlap` + `SceneOverlap` (two core scenes with `Overlap::Previous(0.5)` and a
slide). Nothing outside `components/`, `docs/components/` and `plans/` changed.

## Verification

- `just -f components/justfile test`: 53 unit tests and 15 doctests pass. The gallery tests
  pass, including the new `tests/transitions.rs`:
  - two keyed wipes in one frame get two `clipPath`s with the ids
    `kit-transition-k-left-1` and `kit-transition-k-right-1`;
  - one panel per series is drawn before frame 15 and after frame 29, and both are drawn
    from 15 to 29;
  - a transition at the end of a series makes `inspect` warn.
- The series layout tests check the total length (sequences minus transitions), the start
  of each sequence, the transition windows, skipped and shortened transitions, and that
  progress is 0 at the window start and 1 at its end.
- `inspect` over the whole gallery checks 655 frames and finds no problems.
- `strip Slide -n 8`, `strip Fade`, `strip Wipe`, `strip SceneFade` and
  `strip BeforeOverlap@0.8s..SceneOverlap@0.6s`: during the slide the two panels stay edge
  to edge, the wipe edge stays sharp, and the exiting scene slides out while the next one
  comes in over it.
- `inspect Fade..SceneOverlap@end --pops`: no pop on a transition's first or last frame, and
  none in `Fade`; see the deviations for the findings inside `Slide` and `Wipe`.
- The strip of the overlap warns "text SceneOverlap is cut off by the canvas edge" at frame
  4048. That is the title sliding in from the right, as intended. `inspect` reports it as
  info, and the run over the whole gallery is clean.
- e2e cpu: 15 frames at 0.000%, with the new `fade_50p`, `slide_50p`, `wipe_50p` and
  `sceneoverlap_0.3s`. The existing snapshots are unchanged.
- `just -f components/justfile clippy`, `check-wasm` and `cargo fmt --all --check` pass.
- `just -f components/justfile boundary`: the upstream path list is empty.

## Deviations from the phase file

- `Presentation::apply` takes an extra `id: &str`. A presentation that needs a clip path,
  mask or gradient (`Wipe`) gets an id unique to the transition. It is built from the
  series `key` plus the item index, or from the index alone.
- `Slide` and `Wipe` have a `size` field (default 1920 x 1080), the area in view-box units.
  `ctx.current_video_size` is the scaled output size, not the view box, so the size cannot
  be read from the context.
- `scene_transition(ui, presentation, timing, side, key, content)` takes `key` for the clip
  id and `content` for what it wraps.
- `SceneFade` has no `key` (it makes no ids) and no `Themed`, because a container needs its
  children. `SceneFade::new(ui, children)` fills the fades from `motion.duration.fast` and
  the easing from `motion.easing.standard`. It also has a `rate_func` field.
- `Ui::for_scene` now counts the overlap frames:
  `max(duration_in_frames, end_frame - start_frame)`. A scene with `Overlap::Previous`
  renders more frames than its own length, and its exit animations end with the last one.
  Scenes without an overlap are unchanged, so their snapshots are identical.
- The e2e frame for the overlap is `SceneOverlap@0.3s`, not `SceneOverlap@50%`. The 50%
  frame is after the 0.5 s overlap, so it would not show the transition. The scene before
  it is `BeforeOverlap`, so no scene name starts with `SceneOverlap` except the one meant.
- The prelude also exports `fframes::Overlap`, for `scene_transition` users.
- **`inspect --pops` is not clean on `Slide` and `Wipe`.** The phase file asks for it to be
  clean on the transition scenes. It reports 24 findings, all strictly inside the transition
  windows (Slide 3837..3861 at 3838..3847, Wipe 3903..3927 at 3912..3919, overlap 4038..4053
  at 4043..4049). At this speed, a hard-edged slide or wipe always trips the pop check: it
  crosses flat panels, so each pixel changes once between still frames, which is what the
  check looks for. The check therefore cannot be clean for these presentations. What was
  verified is the intent in the parenthesis, that a transition does not pop at its edges:
  no finding is on a first or last frame, and `Fade` has no findings.
