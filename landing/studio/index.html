ONE WAY TO WORK / THE AGENT LOOP

A small change.
A complete loop.

For example: ask for a short title video, then select its title and say “Change the title to ‘Make your move.’” You can also select a scene or time range when individual objects cannot be selected.

Follow “Hello.” from a selected title to an updated preview. Scroll slowly, pause, or go back.

01 / SELECT

Start with what
you can see.

Click a selectable object in the preview. Studio pauses at that frame and carries the selection into your next agent task.

Object selection depends on the project’s preview worker. A selection gives context; it does not change the source.

02 / AGENT EDIT

Say what
should change.

“Change the title to ‘Make your move.’” Your agent edits the Rust project in a draft working copy.

The last working preview stays visible while the next revision takes shape.

03 / BUILD & CHECK

Put the draft
through its paces.

Studio builds the candidate, then validates it with frame inspection and audio checks. The report includes diagnostics and coverage gaps.

A failed or cancelled build keeps the previous preview. Checks report what they covered.

04 / UPDATED PREVIEW

Meet the
next revision.

A validated edit applies automatically by default. With manual review, it waits for you to choose Apply.

The accepted revision becomes the updated preview. Your video remains an ordinary Rust project.

THE REST OF YOUR WORKSPACE

Your files. Your timeline.
A complete video workflow.

01 / YOUR PROJECT

Open files.
Keep control.

Your video is a Rust project: source, media and a timing document. Open it in your editor, keep it in Git, and bring the tools you already use.

Studio adds a workspace around those files. The project remains yours.

02 / THE TIMELINE

Change the rhythm.
Keep the source.

Split a scene, move the outro, or trim a clip’s edge. Timing edits write timeline.json and update the preview without rebuilding Rust.

Timing edits support undo and redo. Finish or cancel an agent task before changing the timeline.

03 / THE BUILD ENVIRONMENT

An example in.
A preview out.

Install the managed SDK from Setup, then open an example. Studio builds the project and prepares its worker, first frame and audio.

The current preview stays available while the next revision is prepared.

04 / THE FINISHED FILE

Make the video.
Take it with you.

Choose an export format supported by your project’s worker, set the size and frame rate, and render a video file. Follow progress or cancel the export.

From editable source to a file you can share. Export capabilities depend on the project.
